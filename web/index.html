        Estimate from photos

        JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, or GIF; up to 20 MB each and 60 MB per batch. HEIC photos are not supported — export them as JPEG first. Large photos are resized in your browser to upload faster. Photos stay on this device until you submit. You can also drag and drop files anywhere on this page or paste images from the clipboard.

          Tip: fill in the tape measurements below before estimating to attach a tape cross-check to each photo.

          Good photo tips

          • Photograph one animal from the side, showing the full body and legs.
          • Hold the camera level with the cow, a few metres back.
          • Use daylight or a well-lit yard; avoid heavy shadows.
          • Low confidence or an out-of-range weight usually means retake the photo.
