      Good photo tips

      • Photograph one animal from the side, showing the full body and legs.
      • Hold the camera level with the cow, a few metres back.
      • Use daylight or a well-lit yard; avoid heavy shadows.
      • Low confidence or an out-of-range weight usually means retake the photo.
